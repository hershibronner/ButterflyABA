The foundation

What is ABA therapy?

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) is the most widely researched and effective approach to supporting children with autism. In simple terms, ABA breaks meaningful skills — like asking for help, waiting patiently, or handling big feelings — into small, achievable steps, then teaches and reinforces them in a way that feels natural and encouraging to your child.

Done well, ABA doesn't feel like a program. It feels like play, connection, and steady growth.

How we help

🏡

In-Home ABA Therapy

One-on-one therapy in the place your child feels most comfortable. Your dedicated Behavior Technician works on personalized goals while a BCBA guides the plan, building skills that carry into everyday family life.

🏫

Center-Based ABA Therapy

A structured, welcoming space designed for learning and play. Our center gives children the chance to build skills, focus, and social confidence in an environment made just for them.

🎒

School-Based ABA Support

Learning shouldn't stop at the classroom door. We partner with your child's school so they get consistent, coordinated support throughout their day.

🧩

Social Skills Support

Sharing, taking turns, reading cues, making friends — these are hard for many children with autism. We help them practice connection in a warm, low-pressure way, at their own pace.

🌱

Parent Training & Support

You're your child's most important teacher. We give you practical, easy-to-use strategies so progress continues long after each session ends — and your whole family grows stronger together.
