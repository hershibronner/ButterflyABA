Questions? We've got answers.

We provide ABA therapy for children diagnosed with autism, ages 2–21, across New Jersey.

Yes. ABA services require a formal autism diagnosis from a qualified healthcare provider. If you're unsure where to start, reach out — we're happy to point you in the right direction.

We accept most major insurances and Medicaid plans, including UnitedHealthcare, Cigna, Aetna, Fidelis, Amerigroup, Wellpoint, and Horizon. Coverage varies by plan, so we'll verify your eligibility and handle the paperwork for you.

Wherever your child learns best — at home, in our center, or at school. We meet your child where they're most comfortable.

Once eligibility is confirmed and a treatment plan is approved, therapy typically begins within a few weeks. We move as quickly as we responsibly can.

Expert, evidence-based care delivered with real warmth — plus a team that handles the hard logistics so you can focus on your child.

The bond between your child and their therapist matters. If it's not clicking, tell us — we'll listen and adjust until it's right.
