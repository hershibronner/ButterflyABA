How it works

Getting started is simpler than you think.

1

Say hello.

Reach out and tell us about your family and your child. No pressure, no jargon — just a real conversation.

2

We handle the paperwork.

Our team verifies your insurance and guides you through every form, so you can focus on your child instead of the process.

3

We build the plan.

A Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) gets to know your child and creates a treatment plan you'll feel good about.

4

Therapy begins.

A dedicated Behavior Technician works closely with your child at home, in our center, or at school, while your BCBA oversees progress and keeps you in the loop.
