About Us

A little warmth changes everything.

Butterfly ABA was born from a simple belief: every child deserves the chance to grow into who they're meant to be — and every family deserves someone in their corner while they do.

Our story

Why we started Butterfly ABA

We've spent years alongside families navigating an autism diagnosis, and we saw the same thing again and again: parents doing everything right, yet still feeling alone. Overwhelmed by paperwork. Unsure where to turn next. Searching for care that actually understood their child.

So we built something different — a place where children get expert, evidence-based therapy delivered with real tenderness, and where parents finally feel supported instead of left to figure it all out on their own.

We named ourselves after the butterfly for a reason. Growth takes time, patience, and the right environment. Given all three, every child can transform in ways that surprise even the people who love them most.

Welcome to the Butterfly family. 🦋
